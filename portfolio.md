## About

Dr. Ritika Malik is an Assistant Professor at Bharati Vidyapeeth (Deemed to be University), Institute of Management & Research (BVIMR), New Delhi, where she also serves as Placement Officer (Law) and Head of the Law Placement & Internship Cell. Her work spans academic teaching, legal career development, research, and scholarly publishing — including service as an academic editor, peer reviewer for leading international publishers, and convener of national conferences and seminars. Her expertise sits at the intersection of law, management, sustainability, and emerging technology.
